About VAV Pyropark

We are a licensed cracker manufacturer from Sivakasi, dedicated to providing safe, eco‑friendly fireworks for every celebration. Our mission is to spread joy responsibly while protecting the environment.

Safety First 🔥

  • Always light crackers outdoors in open spaces.
  • Keep water and sand nearby for emergencies.
  • Never give fireworks to children unsupervised.
  • Wear cotton clothes while bursting crackers.
  • Dispose of used fireworks safely.
